the fact that either exists.

## Inviting someone

Send an invitation to their email address. It expires after seven days, and
re-sending replaces the previous one rather than issuing a second live
invitation.

## Suspending an account

A suspended member keeps their history and their content, and cannot sign in.
This is the reversible action; removal is not.

## Removing someone

Removal detaches the person from the workspace. What they wrote stays, attributed
to them, because deleting a person's contributions to punish their departure
damages everyone else's record of what happened.
