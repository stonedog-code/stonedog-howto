A nested directory, so this article's section is `administration-billing` —
directory names joined with a hyphen, lowercased. The host declares that section
under `administration` in its config, which is what makes it render as a child
in the navigation rather than as a sibling.

## Where invoices come from

An invoice is generated at the end of each billing period and emailed to the
billing contact. It is also available here, indefinitely.

## Correcting one

An issued invoice is never edited. A correction is a credit note plus a
replacement, so the original and the correction both survive — an invoice that
can be quietly rewritten after the fact is not evidence of anything.

## Changing the billing contact

The billing contact is separate from the workspace owner, deliberately: the
person who runs the workspace and the person who pays for it are often not the
same, and forcing them to be means sharing an account.
