This one names `User` and `Admin`. Switch the viewer to **Guest** and it
disappears from the navigation — and from search, and from the response.

## Autosave

Work is saved as you go. There is no save button, and there is no unsaved state
to lose.

## Version history

Every save is a version. Open the history panel to see them, and to restore one.

## Why the article is gated at all

Not because the content is sensitive — it plainly is not — but because a
signed-out visitor reading about a feature they have no account for is a worse
first experience than not being shown it. Access control on documentation is
usually about relevance rather than secrecy, and the mechanism is the same
either way.
